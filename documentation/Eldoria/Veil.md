# Veil

#### "She is the breath between realms—the divine fabric through which all Aether flows."

### Synapsis

The Veil is one of The Three Great Principles of the [[Embodiment of principles]]; the invisible, omnipresent deity of connection—the divine medium that lies between Eldoria and the Luminous Aether. She is not a gate, but the air through which power moves, the flowing threads of possibility that bind all things. Through her, Aether is drawn, shaped, and returned. She is neither seen nor heard—only felt, like silk across the soul. All who touch magic, memory, or mystery do so through the Veil, whether they know her or not.
### Appearence

Veil has no single form. She is seen only in moments of heightened Aetheric awareness—as misty threads of light, drifting like gossamer through forests, over ruins, or across sacred altars. These flowing strands pulse faintly with golden or silver light, sometimes shimmering with shifting hues. When called upon in ritual, she may manifest as a living tapestry of Aether, rippling gently with no source, no beginning, and no end.

### Views on Veil in Culture and Society

Veil is revered by mages, mystics, and elemental weavers. Temples are rare—every place where Aether flows is already sacred to her. Her worship is found in breathwork, silent movement, and the stillness before incantation. Some see her as a mother of magic, others as the spirit of mystery itself. Her faithful teach that every spell, realization, and vision is not summoned from power—but gently asked from Veil, and offered in return.

### Homeland

The Veil does not dwell in any plane. She is woven through Eldoria itself, layered invisibly through every stone, forest, cloud, and soul. Some believe her threads become visible only where the boundary between worlds thins—near Voidrifts, Shatterlines, or Spires of Resonance—but in truth, she is always present, like the wind that shapes a flame.

### Role in the balance

The raw forces of the Aether flows through Veil at all times, however it is never affected by imbalance, it simply is the conduit of Aether itself. Its purpose is not to effect the balance, but to share the gift of Aether.

### Myth

When the Astral Light shattered the Aether Core and the raw forces of Vitalis and Entropis surged outward, the world would have torn apart—had Veil not formed. She wove herself through the new world, catching the flowing strands of Aether and suspending them in rhythm. Her threads became the breath of spells, the path of prayers, and the shape of possibility. It is said that she did not speak the world into being—she listened.

### The nature of Veil

Veil is omnipresent, gentle, and inviolable. She is not a willful goddess, but a living presence of divine passage. She holds the potential for all things, without choosing among them. Those who defile her balance with reckless magic may suffer backlash—not as punishment, but as correction. She does not judge. She restores flow. To those who listen, she whispers insights, carrying echoes from beyond.
