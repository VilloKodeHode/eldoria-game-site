### Zyria, Master of the Forgeheart engines

Skyforged Aetherforged Neutral Mortal: 

Aspect: Persevation - Innovation and Equilibrium

Depiction: A radiant Skyforged of silver and gold alloy, engraved with self-written runes that pulse in cyan and golden hues, her chest housing a dual-forged Aether core.  

Myth: Zyria, a master artificer among the Skyforged, created the Forgeheart Engines—artifacts capable of reviving failing Skyforged or stabilizing damaged cores. Her inventions preserved her kind but consumed immense Aether, draining the lands around them.  

Symbols: Two concentric rings encircling a pulsing Aetheric Ember.  

Worship: Honored by Skyforged artificers and mortal engineers who seek perfection through balance and precision. Her name is invoked in forges before creation begins.  

Followers: Skyforged, human artificers, scholars of balance, mechanists.  

Race: Ancestral [[Skyforged]]

_"Let the pattern hold."_

### Folklore

Zyria’s first Forgeheart Engine still beats deep within the Silent Crucible, powering ancient forges that dream of harmony between creation and consumption. Some say that when her heart ceases, the Skyforged will gradually fade with it.
