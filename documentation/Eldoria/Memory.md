# Memory

#### "It remembers what the world forgets—the silent witness to every echo."

### Synapsis

Decay is one of the [[Embodiment of principles]]. Often mistaken as a cause of misery and destruction, but in truth is a principle of liberation through return. It embodies the sacred process by which all things—no matter how grand or beautiful—must one day break down and return to source. Its touch is slow, inevitable, and merciful. Through it, the old is undone, the broken finds rest, and the buried gives rise to new life. It is the graceful end, and in its wake, the world breathes again.

Memory is one of the [[Embodiment of principles]]. A divine principle of remembrance, resonance, and consequence. It holds the imprint of all things—every name spoken, every vow broken, every breath drawn. Where Harmony preserves structure and Chaos releases form, Memory preserves meaning. Her presence ensures that the past shapes the present, and that the truths buried by time are never erased. She is not merely the past—she is its echo, walking through the now.

### Appearence

Decay is most often seen as a veiled woman of stone and moss, her body eroded yet whole. Her hair flows like withered vines, and flowers bloom where her feet fall. Her voice is soft and slow, echoing like roots moving through soil. In sacred visions, she appears as a funeral of color—rust, gold, umber, and deep green. Her breath smells of turned earth and rain.

### Views on Decay in Culture and Society

Those who worship Decay are mourners, gardeners, healers of old wounds, and grave-tenders. Her sects are often misunderstood—seen as morbid—but their rites are solemn and kind. They tend Aetheric compost grounds, where corrupted magic is broken down. Artists may burn their greatest works in her name to make way for new visions. Among Verdwalkers and Stonekin, she is revered as "the one who ensures nothing is wasted".

### Homeland

The embodiment of Decay is said to dwell in the Evershrine of the Broken Root, a grove where every fallen thing is given a second stillness. Trees there never grow upright, but blossom as they fall. Streams run backward, returning to ancient springs. It is a place of peace—not untouched by time, but perfectly touched by it.

### Role in the balance

Brings entropis under control through rebirth of matter.

### Myth

In the time after the Shattering, when Aether still surged wild, it was Decay who stepped forward to calm its overflow. Where Chaos tore and Harmony preserved, she did neither—she gathered the broken, laid it to rest, and fed the soil of becoming. It is said she once embraced a dying god and whispered, “Now, become what you were before.” From his remains, a thousand new spirits bloomed.

### The nature of Harmony

Decay is quiet, kind, and unflinching. She does not grieve endings—she honors them. To her, nothing truly ends; it is only given back. She is the breath after death, the sigh before new growth, the caretaker of sacred rot. She rarely speaks, but when she does, her words linger like scent on the wind. Rebirth is not her opposite—it is her promise.
