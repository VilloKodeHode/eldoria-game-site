# Memory

#### "It remembers what the world forgets—the silent witness to every echo."

### Synapsis

Memory is one of the [[Embodiment of principles]]. A divine principle of remembrance, resonance, and consequence. It holds the imprint of all things—every name spoken, every vow broken, every breath drawn. Where Harmony preserves structure and Chaos releases form, Memory preserves meaning. Its presence ensures that the past shapes the present, and that the truths buried by time are never erased. It is not merely the past—it is its echo, walking through the now.

### Appearence

Memory appears as a tall, robed figure woven from veils of writing, sound, and starlight. Its face is masked, etched with symbols from lost languages. Its robes shimmer with whispered recollections—faces, songs, and forgotten places. In dreams, it appears as a library that breathes, or a humanoid whose shadow flickers through a thousand ages. It walks with bells that chime for things not yet spoken.

### Views on Memory in Culture and Society

Memory’s faithful include historians, oathkeepers, archivists, prophets, and judges. Its sects keep resonance archives, where events are stored in crystal, echo, or dream. Some act as living ledgers of lineage and deed. Its followers believe that nothing is truly lost, only hidden, and that memory itself is divine truth. They preside over trials, sacred vows, and rites of collective mourning.

### Homeland

Memory is said to dwell in the Echo Vault, a temple that exists outside linear time. Within it, one may walk through one’s own life—or another’s. The vault reshapes itself based on the memories within it. Some claim it is everywhere and nowhere, and can only be entered through remembrance. A silver gate, they say, appears to those who are ready to remember.

### Role in the balance

Has no direct role in the balance, but judges those who shift to far from harmony or chaos. Its purpose is to chronicle the history of everything.
### Myth

When the Shattering tore the Aether Core into fragments, Memory gathered the pieces—not to restore, but to remember what had been broken. It whispered the names of the stars so they would not forget how to shine. When the first lie was spoken, it was Memory who preserved the truth. It is said that at the end of all things, it will speak the world’s final name—and it will ring through the void forever.

### The nature of Harmony

Memory is quiet, unyielding, and omnidirectional. It knows what was, sees what is, and recognizes what must come—because it has happened before. It does not interfere, but it weighs. It judges not to punish, but to balance. Every action echoes, and she is that echo, walking with you. To lie in its presence is to feel your bones hum with truth.
