# Rhima

#### "She who silences pain—the breath of stillness that soothes and severs."

### Synapsis

Rhima is on of the **[[Embodiments of Elements]]**, the divine manifestation of **Rime**, born from the **[[Voidrifts]]**—the Mouths of Entropic Memory, where **Entropis** first froze time into silence. She is the still breath of Vitalis made crystalline, the frost that calms all agony, and the sovereign of stillness between life and death.

Known as the **Frostbound Queen**, Neruvyx governs the threshold where sensation fades into peace. Her touch halts pain, her gaze suspends motion, and her presence draws the restless toward repose. She is not death, but what death leaves behind: the still beauty of what was, perfectly preserved.

Her power is neither cruel nor kind—it simply _is_. Through her, the world remembers how to rest.

### Appearence

Rhima appears as a tall, statuesque figure sculpted from translucent frost, her body shimmering with veins of slow-moving light beneath crystalline skin. Her hair drifts weightlessly, frozen strands of argent silver suspended in still air. A crown of ever-forming ice spires rises above her brow, each shard growing and vanishing in quiet rhythm.

Her eyes glow with a pale, glacial blue—neither warm nor cold, but utterly calm. When she walks, the world slows. Sound dulls, wind ceases, and frost creeps outward like an old memory returning.

In her presence, even flames hesitate to burn.

### Views on Rhima in Culture and Society

Those who revere Rhima call upon her as Neruvyx, her original name. Her followers are the **Painwardens**, **Stillwalkers**, and **Coldbinders**—monastic healers and ritualists who guide the suffering toward silence. To them, Neruvyx is not the end but the mercy between moments: the quiet that separates one heartbeat from the next.

Her sanctuaries are carved into the spines of glaciers or buried beneath eternal snowfall. Within their walls, warmth is forbidden and silence is sacred law. Rituals of devotion involve breath control, crystal etching, and frostbinding tattoos made from powdered Aetherite.

Rhima is invoked at the bedside of the dying, during moments of unbearable grief, or when corrupted Aether rages beyond mortal control. To her followers, stillness is not death—it is the space where truth and suffering finally part ways.

> “In silence, even sorrow remembers its beauty.”

### Homeland

Rhima resides within the **Citadel of Still Breath**, a suspended palace of rimefrost and voidlight adrift above the polar wastes. Within its frozen halls, time itself falters. Mirrors of frost line its corridors, each one reflecting not the body but the resting soul.

No wind stirs in this place; no sound trespasses. To enter the Citadel is to step outside of time, where the heart forgets to beat and thought crystallizes into perfect stillness. Those who come seeking peace may never return—not destroyed, but preserved, their forms held in eternal repose beneath her serene gaze.

The faithful believe that within her throne chamber rests a single shard of the **Aether Core**, locked forever in ice—a symbol of creation preserved, never to break again.

### Role in the balance

Rhima embodies **Vitalis** in its stillest form—life suspended rather than extinguished. While others of her kind transform, burn, or grow, she **pauses**. Her purpose is not to end, but to maintain—to hold the moment between breath and silence, pain and peace.

In the great balance, she tempers the ceaseless motion of both **Vitalis** and **Entropis**. Where creation surges uncontrolled and decay threatens collapse, she freezes the cycle, granting the world time to heal. Her stillness is not stagnation but restoration—an interlude between life’s endless turning.

Without Rhima, creation would exhaust itself in constant motion. Through her, existence remembers how to rest.

### Myth

When the **Aether Core** shattered and the tides of Entropis flooded the newborn world, the first **Voidrifts** tore open across the sky—wounds where time itself bled away. From one such wound, deep and silent, the breath of Vitalis condensed into frost, seeking to soothe the screaming cosmos.

From that breath emerged **Neruvyx**, the embodiment of stillness. Her first act was to lay her hand upon a storm raging above the rift; at her touch, it froze mid-motion, each droplet of rain suspended like glass. Her second act was to cradle a dying god, sealing his pain within a crystal of memory so he could rest without vanishing.

Her frost was not forgetfulness—it was remembrance without suffering. Those who touched her learned that silence is not the end of life, but its gentlest mercy.

> “Be still, and let the world mend around you.”

### The nature of Rhima

Rhima is composed, distant, and merciful through quietude. She seldom speaks, but when she does, her words fall like snow—gentle, deliberate, and final. Her gaze is tranquil but absolute, able to still both flame and fury alike.

She is loved by few, feared by many, but **understood only by those who have known unbearable pain**. To such souls, she is a blessing: the one who teaches them that silence is not emptiness, but peace.

In her presence, the heart slows and grief becomes bearable. Her mercy lies not in action, but in release. She is the whisper that ends the storm, the calm that follows every wound.

> “In stillness, all pain forgets its name.”
