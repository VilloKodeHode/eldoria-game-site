# Cinderys

#### “Born of the Ashfounts, where gods once bled fire—I am the ember that remembers.”

### Synapsis

Cinderys is one of the **[[Embodiments of Elements]]**, the personification of **Cinder**, born from the **[[Ashfounts]]**—ancient geysers of living flame where **Vitalis** and **Entropis** first clashed in divine fury.

She is not destruction incarnate, but **renewal through burning**, the sacred purifier that transforms ruin into the promise of rebirth.

Her existence marks the threshold where decay becomes genesis, where the end gives rise to what must come next. Through her, the fire of Entropis finds mercy, and the spark of Vitalis finds purpose.  
She is the **memory of fire itself**, the echo of a world once shattered—and the will that made it whole again.

### Appearence

Cinderys manifests as a woman of molten flame, her form both ethereal and weighty, a dance of embers and gold light. Her body shifts like living glass, glowing with molten veins that pulse to the rhythm of a heart not fully mortal. Her eyes burn as twin furnaces of amber and crimson, their light carrying sorrow as much as fury. Her hair rises and falls like breathing flame, cascading upward in waves that shimmer with ash and memory.

The air around her bends with heat, softening the edges of all things. Embers drift from her presence like petals in slow descent, whispering the faint recollections of what has burned before. Where she walks, blackened stone turns fertile again, and from ruin, life begins to take root.

### Views on Cinderys in Culture and Society

To the people of Kar’Marok and the firebound lands beyond, Cinderys is not a goddess to be worshipped, but a truth to be endured. She is the soul of sacred flame—the presence that devours falsehood and forges the honest self.

Her followers, known as the **Kindled**, are warpriests, smiths, and pilgrims who seek purification in her fire. They do not pray to her; they step willingly into her flames, believing that only what survives the burning deserves to remain.

Among the common folk she is called the Ash Queen, a spirit who walks the thresholds of death and rebirth. It is said she listens through the forges of the world, judging both metal and soul. Even those who fear her concede that without her cleansing fire, the world would drown in its own unchanging shadow.


### Homeland

Cinderys’ domain lies deep within **Kar’Marok**, in a land scarred by divine catastrophe—the **Emberrift Crucible**, a place where the world itself still breathes fire. It is said that here the molten heart of Eldoria broke open during the clash of Vitalis and Entropis, and from this wound she emerged.

The Crucible is a vast and living scar: rivers of crystalline magma flow through canyons of blackened obsidian, their surfaces veined with veins of gold and ash. Floating shards of molten stone drift above the caldera, suspended by unseen aetheric strands that shimmer like firelight caught in glass. Eternal storms churn across the blood-red horizon, half lightning, half volcanic breath, circling the region in a ceaseless loop of fury and calm.

At the Crucible’s heart lies the **Heartfire Maw**, a massive caldera filled with living flame that pulses like the beat of a great heart. The Kindled believe it to be Cinderys’ soul made manifest—a sacred place where the flame itself listens. There are no cities here, only blackened waystations and open shrines built to endure the endless heat. The Aether hums in this land, mutating spellcraft and distorting time. Travelers swear that the fire speaks, whispering of ancient griefs buried in the molten deep.

To stand at the edge of the Emberrift is to feel the memory of the Shattering still alive beneath one’s feet.

### Role in the balance

Cinderys brings the essence of **Entropis** under discipline, guiding decay toward rebirth rather than oblivion. Her flame is the mediator between ruin and renewal, consuming stagnation so that creation may begin anew.

She tempers the violence of change with purpose, transforming destruction into sacred evolution. Where Chaos unbinds, Cinderys reshapes; where Harmony preserves, she purifies. Her existence ensures that the balance between Vitalis and Entropis remains fluid—a cycle of dying and becoming.

### Myth

When the **Aether Core** shattered, rivers of molten light bled into the deep. At Kar’Marok, where the forces of creation and decay met in perfect fury, the earth ruptured into a wound of fire. For seven days and nights, the world screamed, its molten veins exposed to the void. On the eighth, silence fell—and from that silence rose **Cinderys**, the Flame of Remembering.

Her body was flame given thought, her voice the sound of embers breathing. The first mortals who saw her claimed that her every step left ash that bloomed into life, that she wept molten tears not of pain but of remembrance. To them she said,

> “Nothing is lost that is willing to burn.”

From that moment onward, the people of Kar’Marok named her the Cinderheart, the one who carries both the sorrow of endings and the promise of beginnings.

### The nature of Cinderys

Cinderys is flame made sentient, the will of transformation given form. She is neither god nor elemental, but something between—a sentient scar upon the fabric of Eldoria, alive with empathy and fire.

Her demeanor is calm as smoldering coals, yet within her burns the wrath of ten thousand suns. She walks among the broken and the shamed, the forgotten and the reforged, seeing in them the same fracture that birthed her. To meet her is to be judged not by morality, but by truth: what in you still burns, and what deserves to turn to ash.

Her voice is soft, her gaze unbearable. Those who face her often emerge transformed—or not at all. For to stand before Cinderys is to understand the terrible mercy of her flame: that every soul must one day burn, and that in doing so, it might finally be made whole.

> “From ash, all things begin anew.”
