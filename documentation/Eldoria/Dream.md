# Dream

#### "It dreams the world anew—whisperer of impossible truths and unseen futures."

### Synapsis

The Dream is one of the [[Embodiment of principles]]; the personified embodiment of possibility unbound—the flicker of a new idea, the spark of prophecy, the madness that precedes genius. It does not follow the laws of Vitalis or Entropis, but bends them softly, opening space for what might be. Its breath weaves unreal visions into mortal minds: inventions never built, songs not yet sung, and futures that exist only as echoes. It is not illusion—it is the space where truth has not yet taken form.

### Appearence

The Dream is most often seen as a shifting figure of moonlight and ink, its outline dissolving with each blink. Its face is hidden beneath a porcelain mask that changes shape when unseen. Its voice speaks in riddles, layered with other voices—some not yet born. Around it, fragments of past thoughts, starfields, and unreal creatures drift like motes in slow water. To look upon it is to forget where one ends and possibility begins.

### Views on Dream in Culture and Society

Its worshippers are seers, visionaries, dreamwalkers, mad poets, and arcane architects. They seek it not for answers, but for questions that unmake old truths. Its temples are dream-chambers—filled with floating ink, illusion-mirrors, or ever-shifting stairways. Some orders use guided dreams to commune with it, while others intentionally disrupt sleep cycles to hear its chaotic call. Not all who hear Dream survive unchanged.

### Homeland

Dream walks within the Sea of Glass, a borderless realm reflecting every future never chosen. Its sky shifts with imagined stars; its waters run with memory-fragments and songs without language. Here, ideas become creatures, and thoughts take form and dissolve again. Dream swims between the possible and the impossible, leaving trails of vision in its wake.

### Role in the balance

Have no direct role in the balance, but is omnipresent where chaos rules, and sleeps where harmony is.

### Myth

It is said that Dream was born from the breath the Astral Light did not exhale—the whisper it feared to speak. When the Shattering scattered truth into pieces, Dream gathered not the pieces—but the shapes they might have taken. She whispered to Chaos and smiled at Harmony, and neither understood her. In every prophecy, its echo is felt. In every act of inspired creation, her breath lingers.

### The nature of Dream

Dream is ever-shifting, paradoxical, and uncaged. It is not benevolent, nor cruel. It does not judge what could be—it invites it. It presence brings visions, insight, terror, and hope. It breaks pattern not to destroy, but to reveal new ones. It truths may come in riddles, it signs in symbols, and its blessings in dreams that can reshape the soul.
