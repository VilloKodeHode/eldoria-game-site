# Harmony

#### "The still flame, the quiet resonance — preserver and shielder of what must remain."

### Synapsis

Harmony is one of The Three Great Principles of the [[Embodiment of principles]], born in the aftermath of the Shattering. It is not a being of peace, but of preservation—an eternal stabilizing force that seeks to mend what was broken, to bind chaos, and to hold the fragile threads of reality in place. Formed from the radiant resonance left by the Astral Light, it is the divine embodiment of equilibrium, memory, and the will to endure.

### Appearence

Harmony rarely takes physical form, but when it does, it is seen as a towering presence of crystalline light, veiled in flowing radiance. Its shape is never fully defined—often cloaked in robes of translucent gold and silver, refracting fragments of the Aether around it. Its voice is not heard, but felt—resonating directly within the soul as calm, luminous pressure. Wherever it manifests, the air becomes still, and time seems to pause.

### Views on Harmony in Culture and Society

All over Eldoria Harmony is revered as "Protecting Mother", personifying it as a humanoid mother. Worshippers of Harmony form monastic orders, silent choirs, or memory-keeping sects. She is often venerated by scholars, historians, architects, healers, and those who seek to preserve the balance of Vitalis and Entropis. Some factions believe Harmony forbids change, and thus see her as a force of stagnation—but to others, she is the only thing preventing the collapse of all things.

### Homeland

Harmony is said to reside in the Sanctum of Still Light, a metaphysical bastion floating beyond the reach of entropy. This unreachable realm reflects perfect, unchanging geometry—a palace of echoes, where time flows in circles and the Astral Light’s resonance is preserved in crystalline song.

### Role in the balance

Harmony strives after pure balance with no influence of vitalis or entropis.

### Myth

Legends say that when the Astral Light fractured after the Shattering, Harmony emerged from the radiant remnants left in its wake. Unlike Chaos, it did not tear itself into being—it crystallized from what the Astral Light left behind. It is said that Harmony sang the first Resonance Hymn, a sound so powerful it calmed the first Aetheric Storms and sealed the wounds of the world.

### The nature of Harmony

Harmony is a being of resistance and memory, embodying the urge to preserve form, continuity, and beauty. It is not gentle—its will is firm, often unyielding. It mends the fractures of the world, binds runaway Aether, and keeps the Aetheric currents from unraveling. But its greatest flaw is its fear of letting go. In its embrace of stability, it risks strangling change itself.
